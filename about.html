About

I am an Economics PhD Candidate affiliated with the University of Lille (LEM-CNRS 9221), IÉSEG School of Management, and the IFLAME research center. I hold a Master's degree in Public Policies from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and a dual Bachelor's degree in Economics from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and Cairo University.

My research focuses on the economic history of migration, historical demography, and labor economics. I primarily use applied econometrics and spatial analysis to study historical migration flows, migrant integration, and the cross-validation of crowd-sourced genealogical datasets with census sources.
